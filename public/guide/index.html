How to play

Find the doors with an orange ribbon, collect their secret words, and climb the ranks.

About 5 minutes to read everything. The quick version takes 30 seconds. Tap a section to open it.

The quick version

Trick or treating? Open the app and tap "Hunt for candy". Go to a floor on the list and find the orange ribbon. Knock, ask for the secret word, type it in, and tap "Stamp".

Giving out candy? Open the app and tap "I have an orange ribbon". Enter your door. Hang your ribbon, and give each visitor candy and your secret word.

Lost the link? It is in your building group chat and on the poster at reception.

Open the game
1. Open the game
  1. Tap the link in your building group chat, or scan the QR code on the reception poster.
  2. Enter the building code if the app asks for it. The code is in the chat and on the poster.
  3. Add the app to your home screen before you continue. This keeps your progress in one place.

Add it to your home screen

On an iPhone:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Open the app from your home screen.

On an Android phone:

  1. Tap the menu in Chrome.
  2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Open the app from your home screen.

If you open a link inside WhatsApp, open it in your phone browser first. The steps above do not work inside WhatsApp.

2. Trick or treating: how to hunt

Start your team

  1. Tap "Hunt for candy".
  2. Read your team name. The app makes one up, so you never enter a real name.
  3. Tap "Give us a new team name" if you want a different one.

Find a door

  1. Scroll down to "Doors to find". Doors are grouped by tower and floor.
  2. Choose a floor. The heading shows how many doors are left there.
  3. Go to that tower and floor.
  4. Look for the door with the orange ribbon. Read the door's hint if it has one.

Most doors show a funny secret name and no number, so the ribbon is how you find them.

Collect the secret word

  1. Knock on the door with the ribbon.
  2. Say trick or treat, and ask for the secret word.
  3. Find that door's secret name in the app.
  4. Type the word in the box. Capital letters do not matter.
  5. Tap "Stamp".

The door then shows "Word collected", and your count and rank go up.

If the word does not work

  1. Check that you chose the card with the correct secret name.
  2. Ask the resident to repeat the word.
  3. Type it again and tap "Stamp".

Too many wrong words in a row make the app pause your team for up to ten minutes.

Doors that say "Out of candy"

The resident has run out. Skip that door. It no longer counts toward your total unless you already collected it.

3. Trick or treating: add a door you found

Found a ribbon that is not on the list? Add it so the next family can go straight there.

  1. Tap "Found a ribbon? Add the door".
  2. Enter the tower, the floor and the door number.
  3. Tap "Add this door".

Visit a door a neighbor added

These doors show their number and "Found by a neighbor". They have no secret word.

  1. Go to the door and knock.
  2. Tap "We visited" to count it.
  3. Tap "No ribbon here" if there is no ribbon or no candy. This hides the door for everyone.

If the resident later signs up, their number is replaced by a secret name.

4. Giving out candy: sign up your door
  1. Tap "I have an orange ribbon". If you are already hunting, tap "We are giving out candy".
  2. Enter your tower, floor and apartment number.
  3. Add a hint for hunters if you want one, such as "Right off the elevator".
  4. Check "We have a nut-free option" only if you do.
  5. Tap "Give me a secret name".

On the night

  1. Hang your orange ribbon where hunters can see it.
  2. Keep the app open, or remember your secret word.
  3. Give each visitor candy and your secret word.

Buttons on your door screen

  1. Tap "We are out of candy" when you run out. Hunters then see your door as out of candy.
  2. Tap "We have candy again" if you restock.
  3. Tap "Open this door on another phone" to send your door to a partner's phone.
  4. Tap "Take the kids hunting too" to start a team on the same phone.
  5. Tap "Remove my door" to leave the hunt. Neighbors cannot then add your door by number.

Using a different phone later? Use the door link from your first phone, or ask the organizer.

5. Ranks, ratings and winning

Your rank rises with the number of doors you collect.

Doors collectedRank
0Fresh Ghost
1Hallway Creeper
3Elevator Phantom
6Ribbon Hunter
10Night Terror
Every door on the listRuler of the Ribbons

"Top hunters" lists the 10 teams with the most doors. If two teams tie, the team that reached that count first is higher.

Rate a door you visited

  1. Collect the door's word, or tap "We visited".
  2. Tap 1 to 5 stars under "Rate the candy".
  3. Tap 1 to 5 stars under "Rate the host costume".

You can change your stars at any time. The app does not show who gave them.

"Best candy" and "Best host costumes" show the top five doors. A door appears only after at least 3 ratings and with an average of 3 stars or more. This makes it harder to guess who gave a rating, and a low score is never shown.

Give a costume star (for residents)

  1. Open your door screen after a team collects your word.
  2. Find the team under "Costume stars".
  3. Tap "Give a star".

Each door can give each team one star. "Best kid costumes" shows the five teams with the most stars. Kids get stars only, never a low score.

6. For parents: safety and food allergies
  1. Stay with your children for the whole hunt.
  2. Knock only on doors with an orange ribbon.
  3. Check the label on every treat before your child eats it.

Nut-free option

  1. Tap "Show nut-free options only" to filter the list.
  2. Tap it again to see all doors.
7. Hidden tricks and display options

Type these as a secret word on any door. They do not use up a stamp.

  1. Type TREAT and tap "Stamp" to make candy rain.
  2. Type TRICK and tap "Stamp" to turn the building upside down.

There are two more. Tap the title five times quickly to wake the bats. Type BOO at a door where it is the wrong word, and see what floats by.

Light mode

  1. Tap "Light mode" at the top right if the dark screen is hard to read.
  2. Tap "Dark mode" to switch back.
8. Your privacy

What other families see

  1. A door that signs up shows its secret name, tower, floor and hint.
  2. A door a neighbor added shows its tower, floor and door number.
  3. Teams show only their made-up name.
  4. Top lists show secret names, team names and averages. They never show who rated.

What the organizer sees

The organizer can see every door's apartment number and secret word.

What the app counts

It counts anonymous activity, such as visits, stamps and screens viewed, using a random ID saved on your phone. It does not ask for names, and it does not store IP addresses.

Please keep the link inside the building.
